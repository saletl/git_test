Privacy notice

The Curious Creature Clinic, created by The Burrow Parenting.

The short version

  • Children play without accounts, names, ages, or any personal details.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts in the game.
  • The game saves only which coloring pages were earned and two settings, on this device.
  • Email delivery and the newsletter are not connected yet. An address typed into the email preview is never sent or saved.

What the game stores on your device

To keep the coloring book between visits, the game saves a small record in your browser's local storage: which of the four coloring pages have been earned, and whether sound and calm mode are on. It does not store names, ages, health information, or anything about how a child played. This record never leaves your device. You can clear it with "Start a fresh coloring book" in the grown-up area, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. The game uses no cookies.

Downloading or printing the coloring book

The PDF is made on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no email address is needed.

Emailing the coloring book (not available yet)

The grown-up area shows a preview of how emailing the coloring book will work. In this version it checks that the address looks right, on your device, and then explains that email isn't connected yet. Nothing is sent to us or to any email or newsletter service, and the address is not saved. This notice will be updated, and the services named, before email delivery or the newsletter is switched on.

Hosting

The game is hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify receives basic technical information needed to deliver the site, such as your internet (IP) address and browser type, and may keep short-term server logs for security and reliability.

Children's privacy

This game is made for young children, so it is designed to collect as little as possible. Children are never asked for an email address or any other personal information, and marketing appears only in the grown-up area, behind a grown-up check. If you believe a child has entered personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
