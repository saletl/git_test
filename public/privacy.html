Privacy notice

The Curious Creature Clinic, created by The Burrow Parenting.

The short version

  • Children play without accounts, names, ages, or any personal details.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts in the game.
  • The game saves only the sticker book and two settings, on this device.
  • An email address is used only if a grown-up chooses to email the sticker book or join the newsletter, in the grown-up area.

What the game stores on your device

To keep the sticker book between visits, the game saves a small record in your browser's local storage: which stickers have been earned, where they were placed on the page, and whether sound and calm mode are on. It does not store names, ages, health information, or anything about how a child played. This record never leaves your device. You can clear it with "Start a fresh sticker book" in the grown-up area, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. The game uses no cookies.

Downloading or printing the sticker book

The PDF is made on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Emailing the sticker book (optional)

If a grown-up asks us to email the sticker book, we send their email address and the list of stickers (for example, "Pip the Dragon in spot 2") to our server, which creates the PDF and sends one email through our email delivery provider, Resend. We don't keep the address afterward, and we don't add it to any mailing list. The email provider processes the message to deliver it and keeps delivery records under its own policies. To prevent abuse, our server briefly keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of the address and of the sender's internet address in memory to limit how many emails can be sent; this is not stored in a database.

The newsletter (optional, separate)

The newsletter checkbox is unchecked unless a grown-up ticks it. If they do, we send the email address and the time of sign-up to MailerLite, the service The Burrow Parenting uses for its newsletter, and add it to a list for people who signed up from this game. MailerLite may send an email asking you to confirm. If you have previously unsubscribed, we leave that choice in place. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter email. We never send information about your child or their play to MailerLite.

Hosting

The game is hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify receives basic technical information needed to deliver the site, such as your internet (IP) address and browser type, and may keep short-term server logs for security and reliability.

Children's privacy

This game is made for young children, so it is designed to collect as little as possible. Children are never asked for an email address or any other personal information, and marketing appears only in the grown-up area, behind a grown-up check. If you believe a child has entered personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
