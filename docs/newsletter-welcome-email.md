# Suggested newsletter welcome email

Set this up in MailerLite as an automation that starts when a subscriber **joins the group "Curious Creature Clinic — Newsletter Opt-ins"**. With double opt-in on, that happens only after they confirm, so people who only asked for the sticker book never receive it.

**Subject:** Welcome to The Burrow

**Preview text:** Thanks for joining us after your visit to The Curious Creature Clinic.

---

Hi there,

Thanks for joining The Burrow Parenting newsletter. We hope the waiting room felt a little lighter today.

Every so often, we'll send you a short note with ideas for the moments that are hard to figure out: big feelings, bedtime battles, doctor visits, and the everyday stuff in between. No lectures, and no one-size-fits-all rules. Just ways to understand what's going on underneath and a few things to try.

If you ever want to talk something through, you can find out more about working with The Burrow at theburrowparenting.com.

Warmly,
The Burrow Parenting

*You're getting this because you signed up from The Curious Creature Clinic. You can unsubscribe anytime with the link below.*

---

Notes:

- Keep MailerLite's unsubscribe link and your mailing address in the footer (required for marketing email).
- Don't mention the child or the game play beyond the general "after your visit" line; the game sends nothing about either.
